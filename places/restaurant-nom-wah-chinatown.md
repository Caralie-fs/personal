NYC's oldest dim sum parlor — dumplings arrive fast, kids point at what they want, everyone's happy.

## The Rundown
A century-old Chinatown institution on curved Doyers St: order dim sum off a checklist and it comes quickly (key with a hungry toddler). Fluffy roast-pork buns, egg rolls, and dumplings are all easy, shareable, grabbable kid food.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** 13 Doyers St, Chinatown — B/D at Grand St, J/Z at Chambers, 6 at Canal.
- **Cost:** Mid-range, casual.
- **Food:** Dim sum: pork buns, dumplings, rice rolls — point-and-share.
- **Facilities:** High chairs (verify); restroom.

## Tips
- Go early/off-peak — the room is small and fills up.
- Buns + dumplings = reliable toddler finger food.
