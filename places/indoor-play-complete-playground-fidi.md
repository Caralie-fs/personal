45,000 sq ft, three levels, inclusive with a dedicated sensory room — the biggest indoor playground in Manhattan.

## The Rundown
A massive multi-level indoor playground at 30 Broad St: soft-play for babies, gymnastics equipment and climbing structures for bigger kids, and a sensory-friendly room. The scale means both kids are covered for hours.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** 30 Broad St, FiDi — 2/3 to Broad St (steps away) or R/W Rector. Transit over car.
- **Cost:** Premium; check current session pricing and whether to book ahead.
- **Food:** FiDi options all around; Stone Street on nice days.
- **Facilities:** Built inclusive: sensory room, elevators, family restrooms.

## Tips
- Pair with The Battery / SeaGlass Carousel — 10 min walk.
- Weekday mornings least crowded.
