The original 1916 hot-dog stand on the boardwalk — fast, cheap, and about as toddler-easy as food gets.

## The Rundown
The original Nathan's at Surf & Stillwell (home of the July 4th hot-dog contest): hot dogs, fries, and crinkle-cut everything, steps from the boardwalk and beach. Not a kids-menu place — it's a hot-dog-in-hand place, which toddlers love.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** 1310 Surf Ave, Coney Island — D/F/N/Q to Coney Island–Stillwell.
- **Cost:** Mid-range, casual.
- **Food:** Hot dogs, crinkle fries, shakes; boardwalk-casual.
- **Facilities:** High chairs (verify); restroom.

## Tips
- Grab dogs and walk the boardwalk with a Aquarium/beach day.
- Order at the counter — no waiting with a restless toddler.
