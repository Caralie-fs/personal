A hands-on children's museum in the South Bronx (Mott Haven), themed around the
Bronx River and nature. A smaller, calmer indoor option than the big museums.

## The Rundown
Interactive, nature-focused exhibits scaled for little kids — water, animals,
making/play areas. Good rainy-day pick on the Bronx side, and less overwhelming
than the citywide giants.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Mott Haven, South Bronx; subway + bus, or a short drive.
- **By car:** Street parking + garages in Mott Haven (≈20–30 min from Williamsburg).
- **Cost:** Moderate; under-1 typically free.
- **Food:** Bring snacks; neighborhood options nearby.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms + changing; stroller-friendly.
