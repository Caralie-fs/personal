Free, recurring story times for babies and toddlers at Brooklyn Public Library
branches in the Williamsburg area — books, songs, and rhymes. A close-to-home,
no-cost, indoor default.

## The Rundown
BPL branches run age-targeted sessions (baby/toddler/preschool). Typically
20–30 minutes of reading, singing, and movement, often with free play after.
Format and schedule vary by branch.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Williamsburgh Library (Division Ave) and other nearby BPL
  branches; walkable/short transit from most of Williamsburg.
- **Cost:** Free.
- **Food:** None in libraries; snack before/after.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms at larger branches; changing tables vary.

## Tips
- Schedules rotate by season — check the specific branch calendar (`verify:`).
- Great rainy-day default; low-stakes if you need to leave mid-session.
- Note: Brooklyn = BPL (this), not NYPL — different system, different calendar.
