One of the country's largest antique carousels, mid-Central Park — a classic,
cheap, quick win you can fold into any park day.

## The Rundown
Hand-carved horses, a short ride, low stakes. Perfect filler between playground
and picnic, and a reliable toddler crowd-pleaser.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Mid-park around 64th St; enter from 5th/6th Ave sides.
- **By car:** No parking — take transit and walk in.
- **Cost:** Small per-ride fee.
- **Food:** Park carts; picnic on the green.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms nearby; bring a changing pad.
