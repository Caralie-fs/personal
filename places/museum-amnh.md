Dinosaurs, the giant whale, and dioramas — a knockout for a 3- and 5-year-old.
A huge indoor day; the kind of place that earns a membership.

## The Rundown
Endless halls: dinosaur fossils (the big draw at this age), the blue whale,
animal dioramas, and the newer Gilder Center. It's enormous, so pick a few
must-sees (dinos + whale) rather than trying to do it all.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Central Park West at 79th; B/C (81st St) and 1 (79th St).
- **By car:** On-site parking garage but it's expensive; transit is usually
  simpler. If driving, arrive early for a garage spot.
- **Cost:** Pricey for a family; check the NY-resident admission policy.
- **Food:** Cafés on-site (variable); bring snacks; UWS options nearby.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms + changing throughout; stroller-friendly.

## Tips
- Map a route to dinosaurs + whale first; bail before the meltdown.
- Members skip lines and can pop in for short visits — worth it if you'll return.
- Rainy-day powerhouse.
