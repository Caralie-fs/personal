Immersive color-themed art rooms ending in the giant blue ball pit — better for the 5-year-old.

## The Rundown
A sequence of participatory color installations (confetti room, dance floor, treats along the way) finishing in a huge monochrome ball pit. Skews slightly older/artier than Museum of Ice Cream; the 5-year-old's pick.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** 251 Spring St, SoHo — C/E Spring St. Transit only.
- **Cost:** Timed tickets, expensive.
- **Food:** Small treats included en route; SoHo everywhere after.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms; limited stroller space — carrier better.

## Tips
- Do Museum of Ice Cream at 3, Color Factory at 5 — don't double-book the same wallet-month.
