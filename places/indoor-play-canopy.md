A bright, thoughtfully designed indoor playspace in North Williamsburg built
specifically for babies and toddlers. The neighborhood's winter/rainy-day
go-to for the under-3 crowd.

## The Rundown
A clean, calm play environment (not a chaotic bounce-house warehouse) aimed at
the 0–3 set, with members-only amenities. Sold mostly as play-session packages
rather than casual single visits — confirm drop-in policy before going.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Around N 11th St & Berry St, North Williamsburg; walkable
  from much of the neighborhood, near the L (Bedford Ave).
- **Cost:** Package-based (5 or 10 sessions); not cheap. Ask about a trial.
- **Food:** N/A — snack after.
- **Facilities:** Restroom + changing; stroller parking.

## Tips
- Best for cold/rainy days when the waterfront parks aren't an option.
- Confirm the drop-in vs. package policy so you're not stuck buying 10 sessions.
- Note: **The Play Lab closed (Oct 2025)** — don't rely on old listings.
  **PLAY Kids Greenpoint** leans after-school/camp rather than toddler drop-in.
