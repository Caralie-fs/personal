One of the oldest children's museums in the country — big, hands-on, and built
for little kids. A strong Brooklyn rainy-day anchor.

## The Rundown
Interactive exhibits across multiple floors: water play, a mini neighborhood
for pretend play, sensory areas, and rotating exhibits. Toddlers can wander and
touch everything. Expect 1.5–2 hours.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Crown Heights; near the 3 train (Kingston Ave) and the
  C (Kingston–Throop). It's a residential stretch — plan the walk.
- **Cost:** Paid admission; membership worth it if you'll be regulars.
  Confirm price and any free hours under `verify:`.
- **Food:** Bring snacks; limited on-site options.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms, changing tables, stroller access.

## Tips
- Go at opening to beat school groups.
- Destination outing, not nap-compatible.
