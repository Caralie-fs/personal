Hands-on museum in the Snug Harbor gardens — the anchor of a Staten Island ferry-adventure day.

## The Rundown
10 indoor + 2 outdoor exhibits for ages 1-12: bugs, block-building, a climb-aboard fire truck, an art workshop, daily storytime, and the 4-acre East Meadow outside. Sits inside Snug Harbor Cultural Center's grounds and gardens.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Free Staten Island Ferry (an attraction itself) then the S40 bus; or drive over the Verrazzano (~40 min) with parking at Snug Harbor.
- **Cost:** Modest admission; check current price.
- **Food:** Snug Harbor cafe options are thin — pack a picnic for the Meadow.
- **Facilities:** Stroller-friendly grounds; restrooms + changing tables.

## Tips
- Closed Mon-Tue — plan accordingly.
- The ferry ride (boats! Statue of Liberty!) is half the outing for the kids.
