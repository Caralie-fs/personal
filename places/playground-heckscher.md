The largest playground in Central Park, near the southwest corner — big play
structures, rocks to climb, and summer water play. A strong anchor for a park day.

## The Rundown
Sprawling playground with varied equipment for both a 3- and 5-year-old, natural
rock outcrops to clamber on, and sprinklers in summer. Room to spread out.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Enter around 7th Ave / 62nd St (SW corner); A/B/C/D/1 at
  Columbus Circle, N/R/W at 5th Ave–59th.
- **By car:** No parking — transit and walk in.
- **Cost:** Free.
- **Food:** Park carts; picnic on nearby greens.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms nearby; bring a changing pad.

## Tips
- Easy to chain with the [Central Park Carousel](attraction-central-park-carousel.md).
- Summer: bring water gear for the sprinklers.
