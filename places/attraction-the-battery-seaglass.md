A waterfront park at Manhattan's tip with harbor and Statue of Liberty views,
plus the magical **SeaGlass Carousel** — riders sit inside glowing fish. A short,
delightful outing.

## The Rundown
The Battery is a big green waterfront park (with a playground and gardens). The
SeaGlass Carousel is the highlight for little kids: bioluminescent-style fish
that swirl — short, mesmerizing, low-stakes. Great harbor views all around.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Lower Manhattan tip; 1 (South Ferry), R/W (Whitehall), 4/5
  (Bowling Green).
- **By car:** FiDi garages are pricey — transit is easier.
- **Cost:** Park free; carousel is a small per-ride fee.
- **Food:** Park kiosks (seasonal); FiDi options nearby.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms + changing; stroller-friendly.

## Tips
- Combine with a Staten Island Ferry ride (free, big boat = toddler win).
- Carousel is seasonal/weather-dependent — check before a special trip.
