The big open-air weekend food market on the Williamsburg waterfront. Stroller-
friendly, lots of space, and endless snack options — an easy weekend outing.

## The Rundown
Dozens of food vendors in an open park setting with Manhattan views. Toddlers
do fine here: room to walk, things to look at, and easy finger foods. It can
get crowded midday, so timing matters.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Williamsburg waterfront (Marsha P. Johnson State Park, the
  former East River State Park); near the L (Bedford Ave) and the ferry.
- **Cost:** Free to enter; pay per food stall.
- **Food:** That's the whole point — wide variety, easy toddler-friendly bites.
- **Facilities:** Limited on-site restrooms; no reliable changing table — plan ahead.

## Tips
- Seasonal and usually weekends only — confirm day/dates under `verify:`.
- Go right at opening to beat crowds with a stroller.
- Not nap-compatible; it's a midday-energy outing.
