Sprinkle pool + slides + unlimited ice cream — a pricey but guaranteed-hit treat outing.

## The Rundown
Not really a museum — an interactive ice-cream-themed funhouse: the famous sprinkle pool, a three-story slide, candy rooms, and ice cream samples throughout. Pure delight for a 3- and 5-year-old; your wallet will feel it.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** 558 Broadway, SoHo — N/R Prince St. Transit only.
- **Cost:** Timed tickets, expensive; look for kids-free or off-peak promos.
- **Food:** Ice cream is included (that's dinner ruined); SoHo cafes for the adults.
- **Facilities:** Stroller check available; restrooms.

## Tips
- Book the first morning slot — smallest crowds, cleanest sprinkle pool.
- Special-occasion pick, not a routine one.
