Williamsburg's oldest bar (since 1887) — a low-key, family-friendly American grill, big on weekend brunch.

## The Rundown
A neighborhood institution on the corner of Berry & N 8th: American bar-and-grill food with a kids menu, and a reliably family-heavy Sunday brunch. Historic tin-ceiling room, casual, welcoming to strollers off-peak.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** 96 Berry St, North Williamsburg — walkable; L at Bedford Ave.
- **Cost:** Mid-range American; kids menu available.
- **Food:** Burgers, sandwiches, wings, brunch classics; vegetarian/vegan options.
- **Facilities:** High chairs (verify); restroom; outdoor seating in season.

## Tips
- Go for weekend brunch when it's most family-friendly.
- Off-peak is easier with a stroller than a packed weekend night.
