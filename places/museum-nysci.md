A big hands-on science museum in Flushing Meadows–Corona Park with a dedicated
preschool area — the Queens anchor for curious little ones.

## The Rundown
Interactive science exhibits, plus a preschool-targeted space designed for the
under-5 crowd. A large outdoor Science Playground operates seasonally. Lots of
room to roam; plan 2 hours indoors, more if the playground is open.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Flushing Meadows–Corona Park; near the 7 train (111 St).
- **Cost:** Paid admission; membership pays off for repeat visits. Confirm
  price and any free hours under `verify:`.
- **Food:** Café on-site varies; bring snacks. Park space for picnics.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms, changing tables, stroller access.

## Tips
- Head to the preschool area first before older-kid crowds build.
- Outdoor Science Playground is seasonal and a highlight — check before going.
- Destination outing, not nap-compatible.
