A sugar-refinery-themed playground in Domino Park, right on the Williamsburg
waterfront with big Manhattan and bridge views. A local go-to.

## The Rundown
An elevated, industrial-themed play structure (a nod to the old Domino Sugar
factory) plus a water-play feature in warm months. The park around it has a
long promenade, lawns, and food — easy to make a half-day of it.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Williamsburg waterfront off Kent Ave; walkable from much
  of the neighborhood, near the L (Bedford Ave) and the ferry.
- **Cost:** Free.
- **Food:** Tacocina and other park vendors (seasonal); plenty nearby on Kent/Wythe.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms and changing tables in the park (hours seasonal).

## Tips
- Nap-compatible: stroller-friendly, easy to come and go.
- Bring water-play gear (swim diaper, towel, change of clothes) in summer.
- Pair with a waterfront walk or the ferry for a low-stakes outing.
