A whimsical, long-running UES institution famous for **Frozen Hot Chocolate** —
a fun dessert-forward treat outing kids remember. More experience than meal.

## The Rundown
Playful, cluttered-charming decor and giant sweet treats (sundaes, the signature
frozen hot chocolate). Order food too, but the draw is the dessert spectacle.
Touristy and can have a wait — manage expectations with little kids.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Upper East Side (near 60th St); N/R/W (Lex–59th), 4/5/6 (59th).
- **By car:** UES garages are pricey — transit is easier.
- **Cost:** Mid-range; treats add up.
- **Food:** Full menu + famous desserts.
- **Facilities:** Restroom; confirm high chairs (`verify:`).

## Tips
- Go off-peak or expect a wait; split a frozen hot chocolate (they're huge).
- Pair with a Central Park afternoon nearby.
