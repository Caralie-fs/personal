A five-floor, hands-on museum built specifically for little kids — a go-to
rainy-day anchor on the Upper West Side.

## The Rundown
Exhibits are interactive and toddler-scaled — water play, building, art-making,
dramatic play, and early-literacy areas. Toddlers can roam and touch
everything, which is the whole point. Plan for 1.5–2 hours before meltdown.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Near the 1 train (79th/86th St); B/C along Central Park West.
- **Cost:** Paid admission; membership pays off fast if you'll go monthly.
  Check current price and any free/community hours under `verify:`.
- **Food:** No full café to rely on — bring snacks; tons of options on Amsterdam/Columbus.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms, changing tables, stroller parking on-site.

## Tips
- Go right at opening to beat the crowds and school groups.
- Not nap-compatible — it's a destination outing, not a quick swing-by.
- Pair with Central Park playgrounds if the weather turns.
