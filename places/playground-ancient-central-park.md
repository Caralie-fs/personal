An Egyptian/pyramid-themed playground in Central Park near the Met — shady,
fenced, and easy to combine with a museum morning or a park walk.

## The Rundown
Climbing structures, slides, swings, and seasonal sprinklers. Fenced with
limited entrances, which makes it manageable for a runner-aged toddler. Plenty
of space to picnic and let them roam.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Enter Central Park around 85th St on the east side; near
  the 4/5/6 at 86th St.
- **Cost:** Free.
- **Food:** Bring a picnic; nothing reliable inside the park here.
- **Facilities:** Public restroom nearby (hours vary — see `verify:`); no
  changing table — bring a pad.

## Tips
- Nap-compatible: easy to bail or let them doze in the stroller.
- Bring a towel + change of clothes in sprinkler season.
- Pair with a short, low-stakes visit to the Met or a Central Park stroll.
