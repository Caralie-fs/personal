East Williamsburg's neighborhood green — playground, sprinklers, ballfields, and a dog run.

## The Rundown
A full neighborhood park (not just a playground): a fenced play area with sprinklers, open lawns, courts, and a popular dog run. Calmer and more local than the waterfront parks.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Around Olive/Sharon St, East Williamsburg — a stroller push or short drive east; street parking usually findable.
- **Cost:** Free (NYC Parks).
- **Food:** Bring snacks; neighborhood options nearby.
- **Facilities:** Sprinklers in summer; restrooms vary — verify.

## Tips
- The dog run is a bonus toddler-entertainment stop.
- Grass + shade make it a good picnic park.
