Design-forward indoor playground with a star-filled ball pit — the artsy Greenpoint sibling of Space Club.

## The Rundown
Run by designer-parents; features a huge ball pit of clear plastic stars, climbing structures, swings, and a CJ Hendry art installation. Often called the most beautifully laid-out indoor playground in Brooklyn.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** 220 Newel St, Greenpoint (near McGolrick Park) — short drive or a long stroller push from Williamsburg; G at Nassau Ave.
- **Cost:** ~$35 single pass (one child + one adult, 2 hours); memberships + sibling discounts.
- **Food:** Snacks for purchase (the Fort Greene location has the bigger cafe).
- **Facilities:** Grip socks provided; lockers.

## Tips
- No reservation needed — a genuine drop-in, good for sudden rain.
- Pair with McGolrick or Transmitter Park on a mixed-weather day.
