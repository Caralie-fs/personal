A big underground food hall — everyone picks their own thing, so the pickiest toddler and the adults all win.

## The Rundown
A sprawling food hall under City Point with dozens of vendors (pizza, tacos, bagels, ice cream). Communal seating and a something-for-everyone spread make it the easy Downtown Brooklyn move with kids.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** 445 Albee Square W (City Point), Downtown Brooklyn — B/Q/R at DeKalb, A/C/F at Jay St.
- **Cost:** Mid-range, casual.
- **Food:** Dozens of stalls — pizza, tacos, bagels, dumplings, ice cream.
- **Facilities:** High chairs (verify); restroom.

## Tips
- Food hall = no fighting over one menu.
- Pair with the Transit Museum a few blocks away.
