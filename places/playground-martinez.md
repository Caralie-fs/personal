A well-used East Williamsburg playground with sprinklers, right off the Graham Ave L.

## The Rundown
A NYC Parks playground on Scholes St with climbers, swings, and summer sprinklers. Named for community advocate Thelma Martinez. Handy if you're near the Graham Ave stretch.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Scholes St between Manhattan & Graham — steps from the Graham Ave L.
- **Cost:** Free (NYC Parks).
- **Food:** Bring snacks; neighborhood options nearby.
- **Facilities:** Sprinklers in summer; restrooms vary — verify.

## Tips
- Combine with Graham Ave errands/coffee.
- Sprinklers run in summer — bring a towel.
