## The Rundown
A lovingly restored vintage soda fountain in Carroll Gardens: classic egg creams, ice-cream sundaes, and simple, not-too-fried sandwiches and soups. The old-timey counter is pure kid magic. Near Carroll Park.

- **Cost:** Mid-range, casual.
- **Food:** Egg creams, sundaes, grilled cheese, soups.
