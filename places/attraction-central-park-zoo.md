A small, very walkable zoo inside Central Park — sea lions, penguins, and the
petting-friendly Tisch Children's Zoo. Right-sized for a half-day with little
kids (the opposite of the sprawling Bronx Zoo).

## The Rundown
Compact and doable in 1.5–2 hours: the sea lion pool, penguins, and the
Tisch Children's Zoo where kids can get close to small animals. Manageable
scale is the whole appeal at ages 3 and 5.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** East side of Central Park near 64th St; N/R/W (5th Ave–59th),
  F (Lexington–63rd).
- **By car:** No zoo parking; Midtown garages are expensive — transit is easier.
- **Cost:** Moderate; the Children's Zoo is usually included.
- **Food:** Café on-site; park space for a picnic.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms + changing; fully stroller-friendly.

## Tips
- Combine with a Central Park playground for a full, low-cost day.
- Time the sea lion feeding if posted.
