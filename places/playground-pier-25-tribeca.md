Hudson River Park's golden-ticket playground — rock wall, water play, a modern merry-go-round, and a huge sandbox on the water.

## The Rundown
Often called the best playground in Manhattan: a rock-climbing wall, space-age climbers for big kids, small slides for toddlers, a modern merry-go-round, a roomy sandbox, multiple swings, and a big water-play area — all on the Hudson, with mini-golf on the pier.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Hudson River Park at N Moore St, Tribeca — 1 at Franklin St.
- **Cost:** Free (NYC Parks / Hudson River Park).
- **Food:** Bring snacks; neighborhood options nearby.
- **Facilities:** Water features seasonal; restrooms vary — verify.

## Tips
- Go early on hot days — the water area packs out.
- Mini-golf on the pier is a bonus for the 5-year-old.
