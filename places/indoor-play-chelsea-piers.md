The sports mega-complex — toddler open-gym sessions plus gymnastics, climbing, and everything else as they grow.

## The Rundown
Chelsea Piers' Field House runs toddler gym/open-play sessions alongside gymnastics, parkour, climbing, and team sports for older kids. More athletic than imaginative — good energy-burn, and it scales with the kids for years.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Piers at W 23rd St — on-site paid parking actually exists (rare for Manhattan); or M23 bus.
- **Cost:** Session/class pricing; open-gym slots are limited — check the schedule.
- **Food:** Snack bars on the pier; Chelsea Market a 10-min walk.
- **Facilities:** Full facilities; changing rooms.

## Tips
- Chain with the High Line + Little Island for a full West Side day.
- Toddler open-gym times are specific — don't show up cold.
