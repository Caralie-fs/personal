A classic south-Williamsburg neighborhood playground — swings, sprinklers, and climbers, walkable from home.

## The Rundown
A solid NYC Parks playground between Grand St and S 1st: play structures, swings, and summer sprinklers. An everyday energy-burn spot on the south side, less of a destination than Domino but closer for many.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Wythe Ave & Grand St, south Williamsburg — easy walk/stroller from the south side; L/G at Metropolitan–Lorimer.
- **Cost:** Free (NYC Parks).
- **Food:** Bring snacks; neighborhood options nearby.
- **Facilities:** Sprinklers in summer; restrooms vary — verify.

## Tips
- Summer sprinklers make it a hot-day quick stop.
- Pair with a Grand St pizza/gelato treat.
