All-day Mediterranean-diner favorite with a real kids menu and crayons at the table — a Williamsburg brunch standby.

## The Rundown
The Williamsburg outpost of the beloved mini-chain: bright, casual, all-day. Family-friendly plates (chicken schnitzel + fries is the kid hit), a dedicated kids menu, and crayons/coloring pages on arrival. Popular for brunch, so timing helps.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** 258 Wythe Ave, Williamsburg — walkable; L at Bedford Ave.
- **Cost:** Mid-range all-day cafe.
- **Food:** Kids menu (schnitzel, eggs); Mediterranean-ish mains for the adults.
- **Facilities:** High chairs; stroller room; changing table (verify).

## Tips
- Off-peak (post-nap, not peak brunch) is easiest with a stroller.
- Crayons keep the 3-year-old busy while food comes.
