The Village's lively square — a big fountain kids love, two playgrounds, and
nonstop street performers/musicians. Great for energy-burning plus people-watching.

## The Rundown
The central fountain is a magnet (kids wade/splash in summer), there are two
fenced playgrounds, and the buskers/chess players make it a show. Energetic and
fun; can be crowded.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Greenwich Village; A/B/C/D/E/F/M at W 4th, N/R/W at 8th St.
- **By car:** No parking — transit; Village garages are pricey.
- **Cost:** Free.
- **Food:** Endless Village options a block in any direction.
- **Facilities:** Public restroom in the park (hours vary); bring a changing pad.

## Tips
- Summer: the fountain is basically a splash pad — bring a change of clothes.
- Pair with a Village treat (pizza, ice cream).
