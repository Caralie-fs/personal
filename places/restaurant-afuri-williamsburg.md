A ramen spot reported to be genuinely toddler-ready: high chairs, room for
strollers, fast service, a kids menu, and a changing table in the restroom.

## The Rundown
Yuzu-forward ramen and rice bowls. Fast service is the key feature with little
kids — food comes quickly, and noodles are an easy win for a 3- and 5-year-old.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** North Williamsburg; walkable, near the L (Bedford Ave).
- **By car:** Street parking is tough; use a paid garage.
- **Cost:** Mid-range.
- **Food:** Ramen, rice bowls, kids menu.
- **Facilities:** High chairs + changing table reported (confirm under `verify:`).

## Tips
- Go early to dodge the wait.
- Fast service makes it a good "we need to eat NOW" option.
