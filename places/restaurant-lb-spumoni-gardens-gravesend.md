A 1939 Brooklyn institution: Sicilian square pizza + spumoni at outdoor benches — pure old-school kid joy.

## The Rundown
Since 1939: legendary Sicilian square slices and creamy spumoni served at outdoor benches. Wildly casual and quintessentially Brooklyn — kids love the squares and the towering dessert platter. ~10 min from Coney Island.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** 2725 86th St, Gravesend — drive (street parking) or a hike from the D/F.
- **Cost:** Mid-range, casual.
- **Food:** Sicilian square pizza; spumoni + ice cream desserts.
- **Facilities:** High chairs (verify); restroom.

## Tips
- Get the Sicilian squares — the toddler-approved classic.
- Pair with a Coney Island / Aquarium day (~10 min).
