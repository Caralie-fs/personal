A big park straddling Williamsburg and Greenpoint with playgrounds, open
fields, a track, and a large summer pool. Lots of room to roam close to home.

## The Rundown
Several play areas plus tons of open green space for toddlers to run. The
McCarren Play Center pool opens in summer (check for a shallow/kiddie option).
A reliable everyday-outing park.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Williamsburg/Greenpoint border; near the L (Bedford Ave)
  and G (Nassau Ave). Walkable from north Williamsburg.
- **Cost:** Free (pool is free in season).
- **Food:** Nothing reliable inside; cafés all around the park edges.
- **Facilities:** Park restrooms (hours vary); bring a changing pad.

## Tips
- Nap-compatible: easy in-and-out, stroller-friendly paths.
- For the pool, bring the required swim gear and go early in summer.
- Greenmarket nearby on some days — check local schedule.
