A serene sculpture museum + garden with hands-on family art programs — calm, and toddler-workable during family days.

## The Rundown
Isamu Noguchi's sculpture museum and garden: quiet, beautiful, and best for kids during its scheduled family programs (a family of four gets admission + an educator-led art project for ~$10). Pairs with Socrates Sculpture Park across the street.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** 9-01 33rd Rd, LIC (Astoria border) — N/W at Broadway, then a walk; near Socrates.
- **Cost:** Suggested/low admission; check free hours.
- **Food:** Bring snacks; neighborhood options nearby.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms (verify); stroller-friendly.

## Tips
- Go on a **family-program day** — that's when it's built for kids.
- Chain with Socrates Sculpture Park (across the street, free).
