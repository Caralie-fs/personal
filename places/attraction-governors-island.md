A car-free island of slides, hammocks, and open lawns a short (fun) ferry ride
away — one of the best full-day toddler outings in the city, in season.

## The Rundown
Ride the ferry (an event in itself) to a traffic-free island built for roaming.
**Slide Hill** has three toddler-sized slides plus a 57-foot monster (NYC's
longest) for the brave; **Hammock Grove** has climbing structures and hammocks;
**The Yard** is a build-with-real-stuff adventure playground with staff (open
spring/summer weekends). Tons of flat, stroller-easy, car-free space.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Ferry from the **Battery Maritime Building, 10 South St**
  (Lower Manhattan), or seasonal NYC Ferry from **Pier 6 / Red Hook** in
  Brooklyn. Free rides Sat/Sun before 11am from Manhattan (verify).
- **Cost:** Cheap ferry fare (kids often free); the island itself is free.
- **Food:** Seasonal food vendors/kiosks; bring a picnic to be safe.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms + changing tables; very stroller-friendly.

## Tips
- **Seasonal** — roughly May–Oct. Confirm the island + ferry are running.
- Toddlers: the small slides on Slide Hill + Hammock Grove are the zone.
- Bring the stroller — it's a lot of flat ground to cover.
