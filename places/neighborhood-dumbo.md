The Brooklyn Bridge Park waterfront — playgrounds, the carousel, and skyline
views. One of the best toddler-day stretches in the city on nice weather.

## Places here
- [Pier 6 Playground (Brooklyn Bridge Park)](playground-pier-6-bbp.md) — free, outdoor, water play
- [Jane's Carousel](attraction-janes-carousel.md) — quick ride, weather-flexible
- [Bubby's (DUMBO)](restaurant-bubbys-dumbo.md) — kid-friendly comfort food, refuel stop

## Notes
- Chain Pier 6 → waterfront walk → Jane's Carousel → lunch at Bubby's for a full morning.
