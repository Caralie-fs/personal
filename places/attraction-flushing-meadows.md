The giant Queens park around the Unisphere — playgrounds, wide paths, the
fountain, and tons of space to run. The free, car-friendly anchor for a
Flushing Meadows day with NYSCI and the Queens Zoo.

## The Rundown
Huge open park with several playgrounds, the iconic Unisphere fountain (kids
love running around it), bike/scooter paths, and room for a picnic. Easy to
spend a whole afternoon.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** 7 train (Mets–Willets Point or 111 St).
- **By car:** Several park lots — straightforward with a car (≈30–40 min).
- **Cost:** Free (some attractions inside cost extra).
- **Food:** Vendors (seasonal) + nearby Corona eats; bring a picnic.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms + changing around the park; very stroller-friendly.

## Tips
- Stack it: [NYSCI](museum-nysci.md) → [Queens Zoo](attraction-queens-zoo.md)
  → playground/fountain. All within the park.
- Bring scooters/balls — there's space for it.
