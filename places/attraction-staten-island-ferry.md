A free 25-minute boat ride with big Statue of Liberty and harbor views — for a
toddler, the boat *is* the attraction.

## The Rundown
The Staten Island Ferry runs 24/7, free, between **Whitehall Terminal** (Lower
Manhattan) and **St. George** (Staten Island). Big boats, seagulls, the Statue
of Liberty gliding past — endlessly exciting for little kids, and a genuine
reprieve for parents (you can sit). Ride round-trip, or use it to reach the SI
Children's Museum / Zoo.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Whitehall Terminal, 4 South St — 1 to South Ferry, R/W to
  Whitehall, 4/5 to Bowling Green.
- **Cost:** **Free.** No ticket.
- **Food:** Snack bar on board; bring your own.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms + seating on board; stroller-friendly (hold on —
  it can be breezy on deck).

## Tips
- Sit/stand on the **right (starboard) side outbound** for the Statue of Liberty.
- Nap-compatible: the hum + motion knocks toddlers out.
- Pair with The Battery & SeaGlass Carousel right at the Manhattan terminal.
