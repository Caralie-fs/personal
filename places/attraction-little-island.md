A whimsical floating park on the Hudson (Pier 55) — rolling hills, lookouts,
lawns, and an amphitheater. Small enough to feel manageable, fun to explore.

## The Rundown
Built on tulip-shaped concrete "pots," it's a little world of paths, hills, and
overlooks kids love to climb and roam. Lawns for sitting, river views, and
seasonal free performances. Compact — a nice 1-hour wander.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** West 13th St at the Hudson; A/C/E or L to 14th St, or ferry.
- **By car:** No parking — transit/ferry. (Garages exist but pricey.)
- **Cost:** Free; **timed entry tickets may be required in busy seasons** — check.
- **Food:** Seasonal food stands on the island; Chelsea Market nearby.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms + changing on-site; stroller-friendly paths.

## Tips
- Reserve a free timed ticket in peak season to avoid being turned away.
- Pair with [The High Line](attraction-high-line.md) — they're close.
