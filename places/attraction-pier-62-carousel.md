A whimsical waterfront carousel with 33 hand-carved New York animals — a quick,
cheap Chelsea treat on the Hudson.

## The Rundown
Hudson River Park's Pier 62 Carousel spins 33 custom hand-carved animals native
to New York — rabbits, ducks, a sturgeon, and a butterfly-crowned unicorn —
under a green roof beside a big lawn. A short, low-key ride that pairs with the
Chelsea Waterside playground and Chelsea Piers next door.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Hudson River Park at ~W 22nd St, Chelsea — C/E to 23rd St,
  then walk west. Transit/stroller only.
- **Cost:** A few dollars per ride (verify).
- **Food:** Nothing on the pier — Chelsea Market a short walk east.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms in the park; stroller-friendly waterfront paths.

## Tips
- Chain with [Chelsea Waterside Playground](playground-chelsea-waterside.md) +
  [Chelsea Piers Field House](indoor-play-chelsea-piers.md) for a West Side day.
- Outdoor + seasonal — check it's running before a special trip.
