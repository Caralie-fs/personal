A tucked-away, naturalistic playground in Battery Park City with a big slide,
rock scrambles, sand, and summer water play — a local favorite that feels like
a hidden gem.

## The Rundown
Designed to feel like a little landscape: a tall slide built into a rock face,
a sand area, and a water feature in warm months. Shady and enclosed-feeling,
great for letting a 3- and 5-year-old roam.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Battery Park City, Lower Manhattan; near the 1/R/W + a walk,
  or ferry to BPC.
- **By car:** Battery Park City paid garages; not a free-parking spot.
- **Cost:** Free.
- **Food:** BPC cafés nearby; bring a picnic for the esplanade.
- **Facilities:** No restroom in the playground — use nearby BPC facilities; bring a pad.

## Tips
- Summer = bring water gear (the slide + water feature are the draw).
- Combine with the Battery Park City river esplanade for a stroll.
