A real aircraft carrier on the Hudson packed with planes, a submarine, and a
space shuttle. Big, dramatic, and a huge hit with a vehicle-obsessed 5-year-old
(and plenty for a 3-year-old to gawk at).

## The Rundown
Walk the flight deck among fighter jets, peek inside a submarine, and see the
Space Shuttle Enterprise. Lots of "whoa" moments and some hands-on bits. It's a
big day — wear comfy shoes and pace it.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Pier 86, W 46th St, Hell's Kitchen; A/C/E to 42nd + a walk
  or bus across.
- **By car:** Paid pier parking lot nearby — one of the few Manhattan spots
  where driving is workable.
- **Cost:** Pricey; some areas (sub, shuttle) may be add-ons.
- **Food:** On-site café; bring snacks; Hell's Kitchen options nearby.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms + changing; stroller access (deck can be tricky/windy).

## Tips
- The flight deck is windy/open — bring layers.
- A 3-year-old may tire before a 5-year-old; have a bail plan.
