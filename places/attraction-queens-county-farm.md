A real working farm in Queens — animals, fields, and a barnyard — with **free
admission and free on-site parking**. The single most car-friendly day-trip
here, and a big hit at ages 3 and 5.

## The Rundown
Sheep, goats, cows, chickens, and pigs on actual farmland, plus seasonal
hayrides and festivals. Wide-open and unhurried — kids roam, see animals, and
burn energy. Grounds are free; some activities/events are ticketed.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Glen Oaks, eastern Queens — genuinely **easiest by car**;
  transit from Williamsburg is a long haul.
- **By car:** **Free parking lot on-site** — the reason this is a great car day.
  Roughly 30–45 min drive from Williamsburg depending on traffic.
- **Cost:** Free to the grounds; hayrides/events cost extra.
- **Food:** Limited on-site (seasonal); bring a picnic + water.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms + changing; stroller-friendly on packed paths.

## Tips
- Weekends have more activities but more crowds; weekday mornings are calm.
- Check festival dates (harvest, etc.) — fun but busy.
- Bring sun cover and snacks; it's open farmland.
