Small, beautifully designed indoor playscape for 6 months-6 years — perfect rainy-day pivot from Brooklyn Bridge Park.

## The Rundown
A 2,200 sq ft custom modern playscape: cubby with ladder, fireman pole, a bell to climb up and ring. Scaled for the under-6 set — cozier and calmer than the warehouse-style spots.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** DUMBO — combine with the Jane's Carousel / Pier 6 loop; F to York St or ferry.
- **Cost:** Session-based; check current pricing.
- **Food:** DUMBO cafes + Bubby's nearby.
- **Facilities:** Small footprint — expect socks-on, strollers parked outside.

## Tips
- The wet-weather anchor for a DUMBO day: Recess → Jane's Carousel (covered) → Bubby's.
