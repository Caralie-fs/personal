The beloved indie bookstore with a cozy kids' reading nook and story times — a
free, calm rainy-day stop.

## The Rundown
A charming local bookstore (founded by author Emma Straub) with a dedicated
kids' section and a little reading nook where toddlers can settle in. Runs
children's story times/events — a free, low-key way to spend a rainy half-hour.
Smith St (Carroll Gardens/Cobble Hill) plus a Brooklyn Heights location.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** 225 Smith St, Carroll Gardens — F/G at Bergen St / Carroll St.
- **Cost:** Free to browse.
- **Food:** Smith St cafes all around.
- **Facilities:** Small shop — park the stroller by the door.

## Tips
- Check the events page for kids' **story times** before going.
- Pair with [Carroll Park](playground-carroll-park.md) or [Brooklyn Farmacy](restaurant-brooklyn-farmacy-carroll-gardens.md).
