The beloved UWS hippo playground — climbable hippo sculptures with summer water spray, sandbox, and swings.

## The Rundown
A shady Riverside Park favorite named for its hippo sculptures, which spray gentle water in summer. Two playsets for different sizes, a big sandbox, and swings for little and big kids. A UWS institution.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Riverside Park at W 91st St — 1/2/3 at 96th St.
- **Cost:** Free (NYC Parks / Hudson River Park).
- **Food:** Bring snacks; neighborhood options nearby.
- **Facilities:** Water features seasonal; restrooms vary — verify.

## Tips
- Summer = water-spray hippos; bring a towel.
- Enter from Riverside Dr at 91st.
