A 64-foot pipefish slide, spray fountains, and split big-kid/little-kid zones on the Chelsea waterfront.

## The Rundown
A Hudson River Park gem centered on a 64-foot wooden pipefish slide into a shaded sandbox, with a jungle gym, spray fountains, and two water-play areas well-divided into big- and little-kid sections.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Hudson River Park at W 23rd St & 11th Ave — C/E at 23rd St, then a walk west.
- **Cost:** Free (NYC Parks / Hudson River Park).
- **Food:** Bring snacks; neighborhood options nearby.
- **Facilities:** Water features seasonal; restrooms vary — verify.

## Tips
- Chain with the High Line + Little Island for a West Side day.
