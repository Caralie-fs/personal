A waterfront park on Kent Ave with green roofs, turf fields, and a playground —
more open space than Domino, good for a 3- and 5-year-old to actually run.

## The Rundown
Big turf fields and lawns plus a playground, right on the East River. Less
crowded than Domino Park, with room for ball games and stroller laps.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Kent Ave, North Williamsburg; walkable, near the L (Bedford).
- **By car:** Limited street parking along Kent Ave — easier off-peak.
- **Cost:** Free.
- **Food:** Bring snacks; Kent/Wythe options nearby.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms + changing in the park (hours seasonal).

## Tips
- Pairs well with a Domino Park / waterfront walk for a longer morning.
- Open fields suit the 5-year-old; playground keeps the 3-year-old busy.
