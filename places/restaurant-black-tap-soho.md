Burgers plus over-the-top 'CrazyShake' milkshakes — a guaranteed kid showstopper (and a wait).

## The Rundown
The original SoHo Black Tap: solid burgers and the Instagram-famous CrazyShakes piled with candy, an instant toddler-and-big-kid jaw-drop. Small and popular, so expect a line — but pure kid delight.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** 529 Broome St, SoHo — C/E at Spring St, 6 at Spring.
- **Cost:** Mid-range, casual.
- **Food:** Burgers, fries, and giant candy-topped milkshakes.
- **Facilities:** High chairs (verify); restroom.

## Tips
- Split one CrazyShake — they're enormous.
- Off-peak to dodge the line; it gets packed.
