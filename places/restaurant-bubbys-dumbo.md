A relaxed American comfort-food spot in DUMBO — pancakes, mac & cheese, the
kind of menu that keeps a toddler happy. Handy refuel after the waterfront.

## The Rundown
Casual and used to families. Solid kid-pleasing menu and an unfussy vibe, so a
loud toddler won't feel out of place. A natural pairing with a Brooklyn Bridge
Park morning.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** DUMBO; near the F (York St) and A/C (High St).
- **Cost:** Mid-range; portions are generous.
- **Food:** Pancakes, eggs, mac & cheese, comfort classics — easy toddler wins.
- **Facilities:** Restroom with changing table (confirm high chairs under
  `verify:`).
