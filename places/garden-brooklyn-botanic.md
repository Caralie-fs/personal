A beautiful garden next to Prospect Park with a kid-focused **Discovery Garden**
for hands-on nature play. Easy, calm, stroller-friendly — a nice change of pace
from playground chaos.

## The Rundown
Wide paths, ponds, cherry esplanade (spring), and the Discovery Garden where
little kids dig, splash, and explore. Low-key and pretty; good for a slower
family day or a nap-in-the-stroller stroll.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Washington Ave, next to Prospect Park; B/Q (Prospect Park),
  S (Botanic Garden), 2/3 (Eastern Pkwy).
- **By car:** On-site paid lot (handy with a car) plus street parking.
- **Cost:** Moderate; check for free hours/days and member perks.
- **Food:** Café on-site (seasonal); picnic areas.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms + changing; stroller-friendly paths.

## Tips
- Head to the Discovery Garden first — it's the kid magnet.
- Combine with [Prospect Park](attraction-prospect-park.md) next door.
