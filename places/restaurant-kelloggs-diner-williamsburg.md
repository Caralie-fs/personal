The revamped 24-hour Williamsburg diner — comfort classics with a Tex-Mex spin, easy with kids any time of day.

## The Rundown
A Williamsburg institution (originally 1928) reopened in 2024 under chef Jackie Carnesi with a Tex-Mex-tinged diner menu: booths, a long counter, all-day breakfast, wings, and comfort food. Diner format = fast, forgiving, and toddler-proof at odd hours.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** 518 Metropolitan Ave at Union Ave — walkable; L/G at Metropolitan–Lorimer (right there).
- **Cost:** Diner-priced comfort food; all-day breakfast.
- **Food:** Diner classics + Tex-Mex (chipotle honey wings, deviled eggs, fried pickles), pancakes, burgers.
- **Facilities:** Booths (stroller-friendlier than tables); restroom; high chairs (verify).

## Tips
- Booths beat the counter with a toddler.
- Odd-hours dining (post-nap 4pm) dodges the brunch wait.
