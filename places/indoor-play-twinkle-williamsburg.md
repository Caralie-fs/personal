Imaginative, Brooklyn-made indoor playground for the 6-and-under crowd — walkable from home.

## The Rundown
A 4,500 sq ft playspace built around imaginative sets: a climbable life-size tree, a firehouse, dragons, a build-the-Brooklyn-Bridge construction zone, and a rope bridge. Aimed squarely at ages 6 and under, so both kids fit.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** 144 Frost St, East Williamsburg — near the Graham Ave L; an easy walk/stroller push from home.
- **Cost:** ~$20 for a 2-hour open-play session; reservations required online.
- **Food:** No real cafe — snack before/after (Graham Ave options nearby).
- **Facilities:** Socks-only (adults too — bring socks); restrooms.

## Tips
- Book the session online before walking over.
- Weekend open-play hours have been early-morning only — check before planning an afternoon.
