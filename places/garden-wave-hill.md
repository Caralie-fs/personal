A serene public garden in Riverdale with sweeping Hudson River views and a
family art program. Calm and pretty — a gentle, low-key day with free parking.

## The Rundown
Gardens, lawns, woodland paths, and big river/Palisades views. The weekend
Family Art Project is the toddler hook (drop-in making). More "wander and
breathe" than thrill-ride — nice for a slower day.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Riverdale, NW Bronx; Metro-North (Riverdale) + shuttle, or drive.
- **By car:** **Free on-site parking** (limited) — easiest way (≈30–40 min).
- **Cost:** Moderate; check for free admission hours/days.
- **Food:** Café on-site (seasonal); picnic-friendly.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms + changing; mostly stroller-friendly (some paths hilly).

## Tips
- Aim for the weekend Family Art Project hours.
- Bring layers — it's breezy on the bluff.
