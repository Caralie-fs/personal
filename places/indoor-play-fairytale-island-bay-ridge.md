Indoor playground + cafe in Bay Ridge — a sit-with-coffee-while-they-play spot.

## The Rundown
Neighborhood playground-and-cafe combo at 7110 Third Ave: play structures for the kids, table service for the adults. A destination mainly if already south (or pairing with Owl's Head Park / the Narrows waterfront).

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Bay Ridge — realistically a drive from Williamsburg (~25-35 min); street parking generally findable.
- **Cost:** Per-child entry; check current rates.
- **Food:** On-site cafe — that's the point.
- **Facilities:** Standard play-cafe setup; birthday rooms.

## Tips
- Far from home — save it for a south-Brooklyn errand day.
