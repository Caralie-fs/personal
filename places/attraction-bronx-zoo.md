A huge classic zoo — animals are an easy toddler win. It's a big-day outing, so
pace it and don't try to see everything.

## The Rundown
Massive grounds with animals throughout, plus a Children's Zoo area geared to
little kids (often a separate ticket/add-on). Lots of walking; the scale is the
main challenge with a toddler.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Bronx Park; accessible by subway (2/5 nearby) and bus.
  It's large — pick an entrance near what you want to see.
- **Cost:** Pricier; some exhibits/rides are add-ons. There's typically a
  pay-what-you-wish day — confirm details under `verify:`.
- **Food:** Multiple food stands; bring snacks and water.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms and changing tables across the grounds; bring the
  stroller for the distances.

## Tips
- Nap-compatible in the sense that the stroller does a lot of work here.
- Pick 2–3 must-see areas and a meeting of the Children's Zoo; skip the rest.
- Go early; midday gets hot and crowded in summer.
