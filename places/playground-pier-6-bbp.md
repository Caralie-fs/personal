A cluster of themed playgrounds at the south end of Brooklyn Bridge Park, right
on the water with skyline views. Sandbox, slides, and big summer water play.

## The Rundown
Several connected play areas — including a sandbox zone and a water-play area —
spread across a large space. Tons of room to roam, with harbor and Manhattan
views. Great for burning energy on a nice day.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** South end of Brooklyn Bridge Park near Atlantic Ave; a walk
  from the 2/3/4/5/R at Borough Hall/Court St, or the ferry.
- **Cost:** Free.
- **Food:** Seasonal vendors in the park; bring a picnic to be safe.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms and changing tables in the park (hours seasonal —
  see `verify:`).

## Tips
- Nap-compatible: easy to come and go, stroller-friendly paths.
- Bring swim diapers, towel, and a change of clothes in water-play season.
- Pair with [Jane's Carousel](attraction-janes-carousel.md) up the waterfront.
