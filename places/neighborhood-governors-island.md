A car-free island in the harbor (a short ferry from Lower Manhattan / Brooklyn)
— slides, hammocks, adventure playgrounds, and open lawns. Seasonal.

## Notes
- **Seasonal** (roughly May–Oct). Ferry from the Battery Maritime Building
  (10 South St) or seasonal NYC Ferry from Pier 6 / Red Hook, Brooklyn.
- Entirely car-free and flat — one of the most stroller-friendly outings around.
