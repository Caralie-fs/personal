An Alice-in-Wonderland-themed tea spot — scones, hot chocolate, and a whimsical
vibe that delights a 5-year-old (and works for the 3-year-old). A fun pairing
with AMNH or Central Park.

## The Rundown
Storybook decor, a tea/brunch menu, and famously fairy "wings" for kids. It's
more experience than quick bite — lean into it as a special outing.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Upper West Side; 1/2/3 and B/C nearby.
- **By car:** UWS parking is tough; transit is easier.
- **Cost:** Mid-range; can add up at brunch.
- **Food:** Scones, sandwiches, hot chocolate; kid-pleasers throughout.
- **Facilities:** Restroom; confirm high chairs (`verify:`).

## Tips
- Can get busy/waits — go off-peak.
- Natural combo with [AMNH](museum-amnh.md) or a Central Park day.
