A museum *in a decommissioned subway station* full of vintage train cars kids
can board. For a train-obsessed 3- or 5-year-old, this is the jackpot.

## The Rundown
Walk down into a real old station and climb aboard restored subway and trolley
cars spanning a century. Hands-on, immersive, and very toddler-popular. Plan
1–1.5 hours.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Boerum Pl & Schermerhorn, Downtown Brooklyn; tons of trains
  at Borough Hall / Jay St–MetroTech.
- **By car:** Metered street parking + downtown garages.
- **Cost:** Moderate; under-2 typically free.
- **Food:** None on-site; lots of options in Downtown Brooklyn / Brooklyn Heights.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms + changing. ⚠️ It's down stairs in an old station —
  **stroller access is limited**; confirm elevator status under `verify:`.

## Tips
- A baby carrier may beat a stroller here given the stairs.
- Pair with a Brooklyn Heights waterfront walk or DUMBO.
