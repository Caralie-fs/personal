A long-running Moroccan spot (NYC since 1983) with a Williamsburg location —
high chairs and a greenhouse-like back room make it a comfortable family meal.

## The Rundown
Tagines, couscous, and a popular brunch. The covered back room is roomy and
forgiving of a wiggly toddler. Established and stable, which is rare for the
neighborhood's restaurant churn.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Wythe Ave, North Williamsburg; walkable, near the L
  (Bedford Ave).
- **Cost:** Mid-range.
- **Food:** Moroccan menu; brunch staples that work for little kids (eggs,
  bread, simple sides).
- **Facilities:** High chairs reported; confirm changing table (`verify:`).

## Tips
- Brunch gets busy — go early/off-peak with a toddler.
- Other walkable family-friendly options to scout: **Allswell** (booths, high
  chairs), **Afuri Ramen** (kids menu + changing table), **Kings County Imperial**.
