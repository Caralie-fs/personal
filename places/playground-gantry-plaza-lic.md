A Long Island City waterfront park with playgrounds, piers, and the best
Manhattan-skyline views around. Close to home — one ferry stop or a short drive.

## The Rundown
Playgrounds plus open piers and lawns right on the East River, framed by the
iconic Pepsi-Cola sign and Midtown skyline. Calm, scenic, and easy with little
kids.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** LIC waterfront; **one NYC Ferry stop** from North
  Williamsburg, or the 7/E/M/G to Vernon–Jackson / Court Sq.
- **By car:** Metered street parking in LIC is usually findable — quick drive
  from Williamsburg over the Pulaski Bridge.
- **Cost:** Free.
- **Food:** LIC cafés/restaurants a block inland; bring snacks.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms + changing (seasonal hours); stroller-friendly.

## Tips
- The ferry ride itself is part of the fun for kids.
- Sunset skyline views are spectacular if you can swing a late-afternoon trip.
