Brooklyn's big park, with a cluster of toddler wins at the "Children's Corner":
the carousel, Prospect Park Zoo, and Lefferts Historic House — plus playgrounds
and endless lawns.

## The Rundown
At ages 3 and 5 you can chain several short hits: a carousel ride, the
right-sized Prospect Park Zoo (sea lions, small animals), and Lefferts (a
historic farmhouse with kid activities), then a playground. Make a day of it.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** East side of Prospect Park; B/Q (Prospect Park),
  S (Botanic Garden), or buses.
- **By car:** Street parking around the park edges; some lots nearby —
  workable on a non-peak day.
- **Cost:** Park is free; carousel + zoo are small separate fees.
- **Food:** Seasonal vendors; bring a picnic for the lawns.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms + changing near the Children's Corner; stroller-friendly.

## Tips
- Start at the zoo/carousel before naps, then graze the playgrounds.
- Next door to [Brooklyn Botanic Garden](garden-brooklyn-botanic.md) for a combo day.
