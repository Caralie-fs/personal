A beautifully restored antique carousel in a glass pavilion on the DUMBO
waterfront. A short, low-stakes treat with postcard views of the bridges.

## The Rundown
One ride is a great quick win for a toddler — and the glass enclosure means it
runs even when it's drizzly or cold. Low commitment: ride, watch the boats,
move on.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** DUMBO waterfront, Brooklyn Bridge Park; near the F (York
  St) and A/C (High St), or the ferry.
- **Cost:** Cheap per-ride ticket — confirm price under `verify:`.
- **Food:** Lots of options up in DUMBO; nothing at the carousel itself.
- **Facilities:** No restroom/changing at the pavilion — use park facilities.

## Tips
- Check the schedule before going — it's closed some weekdays and seasonally.
- Nap-compatible and weather-flexible (glass house).
- Pair with [Pier 6 Playground](playground-pier-6-bbp.md) along the waterfront.
