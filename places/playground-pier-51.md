A nautical-themed water-and-sand playground on the West Village waterfront — pretend boat hull and a bronze-inlay 'stream'.

## The Rundown
A CBS-named top-5 NYC playground: climbing equipment, slides, water and sand play, a pretend boat hull, and a 'stream' with bronze aquatic inlays. Open year-round; water features run seasonally.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Hudson River Park at W 10th St — 1 at Christopher St, then walk west.
- **Cost:** Free (NYC Parks / Hudson River Park).
- **Food:** Bring snacks; neighborhood options nearby.
- **Facilities:** Water features seasonal; restrooms vary — verify.

## Tips
- Pair with a Hudson River Park greenway stroll.
- Water runs in summer only — call it a sand day off-season.
