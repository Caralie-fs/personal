A small riverside-adjacent playground in far south Williamsburg near the Kent Ave greenway.

## The Rundown
A NYC Parks playground near the southern Williamsburg waterfront, close to the Kent Ave bike/stroller greenway. Named after the Jacob's ladder plant. A quieter option away from the Domino crowds.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Near Kent/Wythe in south Williamsburg — along the Kent Ave greenway; a longer stroller push from the north side.
- **Cost:** Free (NYC Parks).
- **Food:** Bring snacks; neighborhood options nearby.
- **Facilities:** Sprinklers in summer; restrooms vary — verify.

## Tips
- Chain with a Kent Ave greenway walk toward Domino Park.
