An outdoor sculpture park on the LIC/Astoria waterfront — free, open, and often
running free family art workshops. Big sculptures + river views + room to roam.

## The Rundown
Large-scale art kids can walk among, lawns to run on, and East River views.
Free weekend family programs (art-making, performances) are the toddler draw —
check the schedule. Low-key and uncrowded.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Vernon Blvd, LIC/Astoria; N/W (Broadway) then a walk, or ferry.
- **By car:** Street parking on Vernon Blvd + a small lot; short drive from
  Williamsburg over the Pulaski Bridge.
- **Cost:** Free.
- **Food:** Nearby Astoria/LIC spots; bring snacks.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms (seasonal); no changing table — bring a pad.

## Tips
- Time it to a free family workshop if you can.
- Pair with [Gantry Plaza State Park](playground-gantry-plaza-lic.md) down the waterfront.
