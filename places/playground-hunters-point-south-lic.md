A standout LIC waterfront playground — climbers, slides, swings, and wide turf with East River + skyline views.

## The Rundown
One of the best waterfront playgrounds in Queens: climbing structures, slides, swings, and open lawn right on the East River, with Manhattan skyline views. Adjacent to Gantry Plaza for a big waterfront play day.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Center Blvd, LIC — 7 to Vernon–Jackson, or NYC Ferry (one stop from Williamsburg).
- **Cost:** Free (NYC Parks).
- **Food:** Bring snacks; neighborhood options nearby.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms (verify); stroller-friendly.

## Tips
- Combine with Gantry Plaza State Park next door.
- Skyline + ferries keep toddlers watching; bring a picnic for the lawn.
