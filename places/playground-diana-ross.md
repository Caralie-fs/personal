A West Side Central Park playground with a giant sandbox and wooden climbing structures — toddler digging heaven.

## The Rundown
Founded by Diana Ross after her 1983 Great Lawn concert; well-kept, with a massive sandbox and lots of wooden structures for climbing, sliding, and exploring. Close to AMNH for a combined day.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** W 81st St entrance, Central Park — B/C at 81st St (AMNH).
- **Cost:** Free (NYC Parks / Hudson River Park).
- **Food:** Bring snacks; neighborhood options nearby.
- **Facilities:** Water features seasonal; restrooms vary — verify.

## Tips
- Pair with AMNH across the street.
- Bring sand toys — the sandbox is the main event.
