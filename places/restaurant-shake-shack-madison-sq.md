The original Shake Shack, in Madison Square Park — burgers, fries, and a park
setting. A reliable, unfussy kid-pleaser when you're in Manhattan.

## The Rundown
Order at the kiosk, eat at park tables surrounded by greenery and a playground
nearby. Burgers/fries/shakes are an easy default for a 3- and 5-year-old, and
nobody minds a loud kid outdoors.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Madison Square Park (23rd St); many trains (N/R/W, F/M, 6).
- **By car:** No parking — this is a transit stop.
- **Cost:** Mid-range fast-casual.
- **Food:** Burgers, fries, shakes (plus dog-friendly patio).
- **Facilities:** Park restrooms nearby; bring a changing pad.

## Tips
- Lines at the original can be long — go off-peak.
- Pair with the Madison Square Park playground / open space.
