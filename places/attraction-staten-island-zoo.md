Small, cheap, toddler-scaled zoo — farm animals, monkeys, and a famous reptile house; free Wednesday afternoons.

## The Rundown
A genuinely small zoo (the perfect toddler dose): farm animals, monkeys, foxes, and a well-known reptile wing. Cheap admission with a free window Wednesdays 2-4pm. Pairs with the Children's Museum for a full SI day.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** West Brighton — drive (~40 min), or ferry + S48.
- **Cost:** ~$10 adult / $6 kids 3-14, under 3 free; free Wed 2-4pm.
- **Food:** Snack stand; pack lunch.
- **Facilities:** Compact and stroller-easy.

## Tips
- Combine museum (morning) + zoo (afternoon) for the definitive Staten Island day.
