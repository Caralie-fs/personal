A neighborhood playground with a turf field and sprinklers in the Graham/Grand corridor.

## The Rundown
A compact NYC Parks playground with play equipment, a small turf field, and summer sprinklers. A local go-to between Grand St and the BQE side of the neighborhood.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Powers St area, Williamsburg — walk from Grand St / Graham Ave; L at Grand St.
- **Cost:** Free (NYC Parks).
- **Food:** Bring snacks; neighborhood options nearby.
- **Facilities:** Sprinklers in summer; restrooms vary — verify.

## Tips
- Good for kicking a ball around with the 5-year-old while the 3-year-old climbs.
