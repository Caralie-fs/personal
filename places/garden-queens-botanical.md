A friendly Flushing garden with room to run, seasonal blooms, and family programming — an easy green-space stop.

## The Rundown
A welcoming botanical garden (rooted in the 1939 World's Fair) with open lawns, seasonal flowers, a bee garden, and family/kids programming. Smaller and mellower than NYBG/BBG — an easy toddler wander.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** 43-50 Main St, Flushing — 7 to Flushing–Main St, then a walk/bus; parking on-site.
- **Cost:** Suggested/low admission; check free hours.
- **Food:** Bring snacks; neighborhood options nearby.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms (verify); stroller-friendly.

## Tips

- Check for kids' programs/seasonal events before going.
