Sharks, sea lions, and an ocean boardwalk — the borough's animal big-day, mostly weather-proof.

## The Rundown
WCS aquarium on the Coney Island boardwalk: the Ocean Wonders sharks building (indoor), sea lion demos, sea otters, penguins. Half indoor, so it works in mixed weather; boardwalk + beach right outside for the sunny half.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Coney Island — drive (~30-40 min), paid lot on-site; or F/Q to W 8th St.
- **Cost:** WCS pricing (a Bronx Zoo membership may cover it — check reciprocity).
- **Food:** Boardwalk classics (Nathan's) steps away; cafe on-site.
- **Facilities:** Stroller-friendly; restrooms + changing tables.

## Tips
- Sea lion demo times set the schedule — check on arrival.
- Summer: combine with the beach; toddlers + sand = the whole afternoon.
