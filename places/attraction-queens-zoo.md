A small, walkable zoo in Flushing Meadows–Corona Park — North American animals
plus a barnyard petting area. Right-sized for a half-day, and an easy combo
with NYSCI and the park.

## The Rundown
Bison, pumas, sea lions, and a domestic-animals area where little kids get
close to sheep/goats. Compact loop — doable in about an hour, low-stress.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Flushing Meadows–Corona Park; 7 train (111 St) then a walk.
- **By car:** Park lots nearby — easy with a car (≈30–40 min from Williamsburg).
- **Cost:** Moderate; under-3 often free.
- **Food:** Park vendors (seasonal); bring a picnic.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms + changing; stroller-friendly.

## Tips
- Combine with [NYSCI](museum-nysci.md) and park playgrounds for a full day.
- The petting/barnyard area is the toddler highlight.
