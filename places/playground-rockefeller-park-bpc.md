A big riverside lawn + playground at Battery Park City's north end — swings, structures, and open grass to run.

## The Rundown
The large playground at the north end of Battery Park City: play structures, swings, and a huge flat lawn right on the Hudson esplanade — great for toddlers who need to just run. Pairs with Teardrop Park a block away.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Battery Park City north end (Chambers St / River Terrace) — 1/2/3 at Chambers St.
- **Cost:** Free (NYC Parks / Hudson River Park).
- **Food:** Bring snacks; neighborhood options nearby.
- **Facilities:** Water features seasonal; restrooms vary — verify.

## Tips
- Combine with Teardrop Park (a block away) for two very different playgrounds.
- The lawn is perfect for a picnic + run-around.
