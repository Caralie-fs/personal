Home of the jaw-dropping Panorama of NYC plus a hands-on art lab and family workshops — free Friday nights for NY residents.

## The Rundown
In Flushing Meadows–Corona Park: the giant Panorama of the City of New York (a scale model of all five boroughs) wows kids, plus a hands-on ArtAccess lab and family workshops. Suggested admission; free Friday evenings for NY-state residents (book ahead).

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Flushing Meadows–Corona Park — 7 to Mets–Willets Point, or park it (lots on-site).
- **Cost:** Suggested/low admission; check free hours.
- **Food:** Bring snacks; neighborhood options nearby.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms (verify); stroller-friendly.
