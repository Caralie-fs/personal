Free, recurring story times for babies and toddlers at New York Public Library
branches — books, songs, rhymes, and a chance to socialize. A reliable
no-cost, indoor, nap-adjacent outing.

## The Rundown
Most branches run age-targeted sessions (baby lap-sit, toddler time, preschool
story time). Typically 20–30 minutes of reading, singing, and movement led by a
librarian, often followed by free play. Format varies by branch.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Pick your nearest branch — NYPL covers Manhattan, the
  Bronx, and Staten Island. (Brooklyn = BPL, Queens = QPL, which run their own.)
- **Cost:** Free.
- **Food:** No food in libraries; snack before/after.
- **Facilities:** Most larger branches have restrooms; changing tables vary.

## Tips
- Schedules rotate by season — check your branch's calendar (see `verify:`).
- Some sessions are first-come, some need free tickets — arrive early.
- Great rainy-day default; low-stakes if you need to bail mid-session.
