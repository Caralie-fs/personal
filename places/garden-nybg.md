A huge, gorgeous garden next to the Bronx Zoo with a dedicated **Everett
Children's Adventure Garden** — hands-on nature play for little kids. On-site
parking makes it an easy car day.

## The Rundown
Acres of gardens, a conservatory, tram, and the kid-focused Adventure Garden
(a maze, giant flowers, discovery activities). Pretty and calm; pairs naturally
with the Bronx Zoo next door for a big Bronx day.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Metro-North (Botanical Garden) is fast; B/D/4 + bus too.
- **By car:** On-site paid lot — convenient (≈30–40 min from Williamsburg).
- **Cost:** Pricey; look for free grounds hours/days and member perks.
- **Food:** Cafés on-site (seasonal); picnic spots.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms + changing; stroller-friendly, with a tram for distances.

## Tips
- Head straight to the Adventure Garden — it's the kid magnet.
- Combine with [Bronx Zoo](attraction-bronx-zoo.md) (adjacent) if you have the stamina.
