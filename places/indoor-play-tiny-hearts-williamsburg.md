A calm, Montessori-style play cafe for ages 0–6 in Williamsburg — soft play with sight lines to real espresso.

## The Rundown
A reservation-based indoor play cafe at 123 Hope St: soft-play zones (slide, ball pit, pretend grocery store) for babies through six-year-olds, with a cafe where caretakers can sit with espresso and full sight lines across the room. Calm and clean rather than warehouse-chaotic. (You asked for 'Tiny Hearts Club' — the venue is Tiny Hearts Play Cafe.)

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** 123 Hope St, Williamsburg — walkable from much of the neighborhood; L at Lorimer / G at Metropolitan.
- **Cost:** Drop-in Open Play (reservation recommended) or monthly membership — verify current rates.
- **Food:** On-site cafe: espresso drinks + treats. That's the selling point for parents.
- **Facilities:** Soft play, socks likely required; restroom; stroller parking.

## Tips
- Reserve Open Play ahead — they cap numbers to avoid crowding.
- The cafe sight lines make it a genuine sit-down-with-coffee option.
