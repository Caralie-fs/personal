Central Park's famous 45-foot granite slide — plus a newer toddler-scaled slide and a net climber built for ages 2–5.

## The Rundown
The big draw is the landscape-built granite slide (a rite of passage), and a renovation added a gentler toddler slide plus a custom net-and-cable climber with platforms, slides, and a rope bridge designed for 2–5s. Rustic and shady.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** E 67th St entrance, Central Park — 6 at 68th St / N/R/W at 5th Ave–59th.
- **Cost:** Free (NYC Parks / Hudson River Park).
- **Food:** Bring snacks; neighborhood options nearby.
- **Facilities:** Water features seasonal; restrooms vary — verify.

## Tips
- Bring a piece of cardboard/wax for a faster granite slide.
- The 2–5 net climber is the toddler zone; the granite slide suits bigger kids.
