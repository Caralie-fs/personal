Classic multi-level playcenter + cafe in LIC — separate toddler zone, no reservation needed.

## The Rundown
Multi-level playground with bouncy inflatables, ball pools, a merry-go-round, and a dedicated toddler area, plus a cafe. Open 7 days; drop-in for open play. The nearest big-box playcenter to home.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** LIC — one ferry stop or a 10-min drive over the Pulaski.
- **Cost:** Per-child; cafe for the adults.
- **Food:** On-site cafe.
- **Facilities:** Toddler zone separated from big-kid chaos.
