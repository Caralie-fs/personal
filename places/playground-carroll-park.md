A beloved little Carroll Gardens park with separate preschooler and big-kid play
areas — and year-round bathrooms, a rarity worth knowing.

## The Rundown
The heart of the neighborhood: a fenced playground split into a preschooler zone
(baby swings, short slides, easy climbers) and a big-kid zone (a large structure
+ tire swing), plus ball courts and year-round restrooms. A great low-key toddler
stop — and an easy bail-off just past the BQE.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** Smith to Court St, between Carroll & President — F/G at Carroll St.
- **Cost:** Free (NYC Parks).
- **Food:** Court St + Smith St cafes/pizza all around; [Brooklyn Farmacy](restaurant-brooklyn-farmacy-carroll-gardens.md) nearby.
- **Facilities:** Year-round bathrooms; separate tot zone.

## Tips
- The fenced preschooler zone keeps a runner contained.
- Year-round bathrooms make it a reliable pit stop.
