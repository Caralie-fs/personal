Three floors (plus rooftop atrium) of ball pits, trampolines, and sensory play — the big-day indoor playground.

## The Rundown
The larger Space Club: 3 floors with multiple ball pits, ninja obstacles, trampolines, a LEGO pool, a beading room, sensory areas, and a rooftop atrium, plus a cafe. Enough here for a whole rainy morning with a 3- and 5-year-old.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** 143 Waverly Ave, Fort Greene — drive (street parking) or G to Clinton–Washington.
- **Cost:** ~$35 single pass (one child + one adult, 2 hours); no RSVP needed.
- **Food:** On-site cafe (snacks/smoothies).
- **Facilities:** Grip socks complimentary; lockers; birthday rooms.

## Tips
- Drop-in friendly — a reliable bad-weather bailout.
- Combine with Fort Greene Park across the way if skies clear.
