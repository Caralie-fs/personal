An elevated park built on old rail tracks running up Manhattan's West Side —
a flat, stroller-friendly walk with plants, art, city views, and water features.

## The Rundown
A long, linear walk with things to look at the whole way — gardens, peek-a-boo
city/river views, public art, and small water features kids can splash at in
summer. Easy to do a stretch and bail. Best paced as a stroll, not a destination.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** West Side, roughly Gansevoort St to 34th; access points
  with elevators (e.g., 14th, 23rd, 30th St). A/C/E, L, 1.
- **By car:** Don't — no parking; Chelsea garages are pricey. Transit/ferry.
- **Cost:** Free.
- **Food:** Seasonal vendors; tons nearby in Chelsea Market (covered, handy).
- **Facilities:** Restrooms + changing at limited points (plan ahead).

## Tips
- Use elevator access points with a stroller (not all stairs).
- Combine with Chelsea Market (indoor food) and [Little Island](attraction-little-island.md).
