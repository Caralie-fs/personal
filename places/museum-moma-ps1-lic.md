NYC's biggest free museum (as of 2026) — contemporary art indoors plus a big courtyard for kids to roam.

## The Rundown
Now free for all (Jan 2026), MoMA PS1 is contemporary art in a former schoolhouse, with a large courtyard for play and summer Warm Up music. More an older-kid/parent art stop than a toddler destination, but free + courtyard space make it easy.

## Logistics
- **Getting there:** 22-25 Jackson Ave, LIC — E/M/G at Court Sq, 7 at Court Sq.
- **Cost:** Free (NYC Parks).
- **Food:** Bring snacks; neighborhood options nearby.
- **Facilities:** Restrooms (verify); stroller-friendly.

## Tips
- Free — an easy low-stakes art stop; let toddlers roam the courtyard.
- Best paired with a Gantry / Hunter's Point playground.
