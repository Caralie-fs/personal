## Free / pay-what-you-wish museum hours
Paid museums with recurring **free or pay-what-you-wish windows**. **These change
constantly — confirm on the museum's own site before you count on one.**

- [Brooklyn Children's Museum](../places/museum-brooklyn-childrens.md) — has run
  a **free weekday-afternoon window** (recently Thursdays ~2–5pm, sponsor-backed);
  reserve or walk in. **(Crown Heights)**
- [American Museum of Natural History (AMNH)](../places/museum-amnh.md) —
  **NY / NJ / CT residents pay what they wish** for general admission (not
  special exhibits). Bring proof of residency. **(UWS)**
- [New York Hall of Science (NYSCI)](../places/museum-nysci.md) — has offered
  **free hours** (historically Fri afternoons + Sun mornings). Confirm. **(Corona)**
- [Children's Museum of Manhattan (CMOM)](../places/museum-cmom.md) — check for
  current **free / PWYW community hours**. **(UWS)**
- [Staten Island Children's Museum](../places/museum-si-childrens-museum.md) —
  ask about **free / discount community days**. **(Snug Harbor)**
- [MoMA PS1](../places/museum-moma-ps1-lic.md) — **free for all** (as of Jan 2026). **(LIC)**
- [Queens Museum](../places/museum-queens-museum.md) — **free Friday evenings**
  for NY-state residents (book ahead). **(Corona)**
- [Museum of the Moving Image](../places/museum-moving-image-astoria.md) —
  **free Thursday afternoons**. **(Astoria)**
- [Brooklyn Botanic Garden](../places/garden-brooklyn-botanic.md) — **free
  weekday admission in winter** + a recurring free window other seasons. **(Prospect Park)**

## Almost-free ferry rides & islands
- [Governors Island](../places/attraction-governors-island.md) — free island
  entry (cheap ferry; **free Sat/Sun before 11am** from Manhattan): slides,
  hammocks, adventure playgrounds. **Seasonal.**
