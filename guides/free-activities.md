Things to do that cost nothing (or close to it). Playgrounds, library story
times, sprinklers, and free museum hours.

## Playgrounds & Parks
- [Ancient Playground (Central Park)](../places/playground-ancient-central-park.md)
  — UES, themed, seasonal sprinklers.
- [Pier 6 Playground (Brooklyn Bridge Park)](../places/playground-pier-6-bbp.md)
  — Brooklyn Heights, waterfront, summer water play.
- [Domino Park Playground](../places/playground-domino-park.md) — Williamsburg,
  waterfront, summer water play. **(walkable from home)**
- [McCarren Park Playground](../places/playground-mccarren-park.md) —
  Williamsburg, big park + summer pool. **(walkable from home)**
- [Bushwick Inlet Park](../places/playground-bushwick-inlet-park.md) —
  Williamsburg, fields + playground. **(walkable from home)**
- [William Sheridan Playground](../places/playground-william-sheridan.md) —
  Williamsburg south side, sprinklers. **(walkable from home)**
- [Cooper Park](../places/playground-cooper-park.md) — East Williamsburg;
  playground, sprinklers, dog run. **(walkable from home)**
- [Martinez Playground](../places/playground-martinez.md) — Williamsburg,
  off the Graham Ave L, sprinklers. **(walkable from home)**
- [Jaime Campiz Playground](../places/playground-jaime-campiz.md) —
  Williamsburg, turf field + play equipment. **(walkable from home)**
- [Jacob's Ladder Playground](../places/playground-jacobs-ladder.md) —
  south Williamsburg, near the Kent Ave greenway. **(walkable from home)**
- [Gantry Plaza State Park](../places/playground-gantry-plaza-lic.md) — LIC
  waterfront playgrounds + skyline views. **(one ferry stop / short drive)**
- [Flushing Meadows–Corona Park](../places/attraction-flushing-meadows.md) —
  huge Queens park, playgrounds + fountain. **(car day-trip)**
- [Socrates Sculpture Park](../places/attraction-socrates-sculpture-park.md) —
  LIC outdoor art + free weekend family workshops.
- [Heckscher Playground](../places/playground-heckscher.md) — Central Park's
  largest playground + summer water play.
- [Teardrop Park](../places/playground-teardrop-park.md) — Battery Park City;
  big slide + summer water play.
- [Washington Square Park](../places/playground-washington-square.md) —
  Greenwich Village; fountain splash + playgrounds + performers.
- [The High Line](../places/attraction-high-line.md) &
  [Little Island](../places/attraction-little-island.md) — Chelsea waterfront
  parks (Little Island may need a free timed ticket in peak season).

## Animals & Farms (free)
- [Queens County Farm Museum](../places/attraction-queens-county-farm.md) —
  working farm, free admission **+ free parking**. **(car day-trip)**
