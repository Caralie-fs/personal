Where to go when it's pouring and the toddler still has four hours of energy.
Everything here is indoor (or weather-flexible).

## Manhattan
- [Children's Museum of Manhattan (CMOM)](../places/museum-cmom.md) — UWS,
  hands-on, the classic rainy-day anchor.
- [American Museum of Natural History (AMNH)](../places/museum-amnh.md) — UWS,
  dinosaurs + whale; huge indoor day.

## Brooklyn
- [Brooklyn Children's Museum](../places/museum-brooklyn-childrens.md) — Crown
  Heights, hands-on, multi-floor.
- [Jane's Carousel](../places/attraction-janes-carousel.md) — DUMBO; glass
  pavilion means it runs in drizzle/cold. Quick win.
- [BPL Williamsburg Story Time](../places/event-storytime-bpl-williamsburg.md)
  — free indoor library story time. **(walkable from home)**
- [New York Transit Museum](../places/museum-nyc-transit-museum.md) — Downtown
  Brooklyn; vintage subway cars to board (stairs — limited stroller access).

## Bronx
- [Bronx Children's Museum](../places/museum-bronx-childrens.md) — Mott Haven,
  hands-on, nature-themed (closest Bronx drive from home).
- [The Canopy NYC](../places/indoor-play-canopy.md) — indoor baby/toddler
  playspace, North Williamsburg. **(walkable from home)**

## Queens
- [New York Hall of Science (NYSCI)](../places/museum-nysci.md) — Corona,
  hands-on science; indoor exhibits plus seasonal outdoor playground.

## Citywide
- [NYPL Toddler Story Time](../places/event-storytime-nypl.md) — free, indoor,
  at branches across the city.
