Where to go when it's pouring and the toddler still has four hours of energy.
Everything here is indoor (or weather-flexible).

## Manhattan
- [Children's Museum of Manhattan (CMOM)](../places/museum-cmom.md) — UWS,
  hands-on, the classic rainy-day anchor.
- [American Museum of Natural History (AMNH)](../places/museum-amnh.md) — UWS,
  dinosaurs + whale; huge indoor day.
- [Complete Playground](../places/indoor-play-complete-playground-fidi.md) —
  FiDi; 45,000 sq ft, inclusive, sensory room. The biggest indoor playground option.
- [Chelsea Piers Field House](../places/indoor-play-chelsea-piers.md) —
  Chelsea; toddler gym sessions (check the schedule first).
- [Museum of Ice Cream](../places/museum-museum-of-ice-cream.md) — SoHo;
  sprinkle pool + slide. Pricey treat outing.
- [Color Factory](../places/museum-color-factory.md) — SoHo; color rooms +
  giant ball pit. Better for the 5-year-old.

## Brooklyn
- [Brooklyn Children's Museum](../places/museum-brooklyn-childrens.md) — Crown
  Heights, hands-on, multi-floor.
- [Jane's Carousel](../places/attraction-janes-carousel.md) — DUMBO; glass
  pavilion means it runs in drizzle/cold. Quick win.
- [BPL Williamsburg Story Time](../places/event-storytime-bpl-williamsburg.md)
  — free indoor library story time. **(walkable from home)**
- [New York Transit Museum](../places/museum-nyc-transit-museum.md) — Downtown
  Brooklyn; vintage subway cars to board (stairs — limited stroller access).
- [The Canopy NYC](../places/indoor-play-canopy.md) — indoor baby/toddler
  playspace, North Williamsburg. **(walkable from home)**
- [Twinkle Playspace](../places/indoor-play-twinkle-williamsburg.md) —
  East Williamsburg; imaginative sets, ages ≤6. **(walkable from home)**
- [Space Club (Greenpoint)](../places/indoor-play-space-club-greenpoint.md) —
  star ball pit, design-forward, true drop-in.
- [Space Club (Fort Greene)](../places/indoor-play-space-club-fort-greene.md) —
  3 floors + rooftop; the big-day version.
- [Recess (DUMBO)](../places/indoor-play-recess-dumbo.md) — small designed
  playscape; anchors a wet DUMBO day with Jane's Carousel.
- [CREA Interactivity](../places/indoor-play-crea-industry-city.md) — Industry
  City tech quest + food hall lunch (skews 5yo+).
- [Fairytale Island](../places/indoor-play-fairytale-island-bay-ridge.md) —
  Bay Ridge play cafe.
- [Sweetspot Playhouse](../places/indoor-play-sweetspot-playhouse.md) —
  Gravesend; rain backup for an Aquarium day.
- [New York Aquarium](../places/attraction-ny-aquarium.md) — Coney Island;
  the sharks building is fully indoor.

## Bronx
- [Bronx Children's Museum](../places/museum-bronx-childrens.md) — Mott Haven,
  hands-on, nature-themed (closest Bronx drive from home).

## Queens
- [New York Hall of Science (NYSCI)](../places/museum-nysci.md) — Corona,
  hands-on science; indoor exhibits plus seasonal outdoor playground.
- [Kanga's Indoor Playcenter & Cafe](../places/indoor-play-kangas-lic.md) —
  LIC; drop-in playcenter + cafe, one ferry stop away.
- [Peekaboo Playground](../places/indoor-play-peekaboo-lic.md) — LIC; castle
  playspace capped at age 7.
- [Bounce 'N Play](../places/indoor-play-bounce-n-play-astoria.md) — Astoria;
  aim for the 6-and-under sessions.

## Staten Island
- [Staten Island Children's Museum](../places/museum-si-childrens-museum.md) —
  Snug Harbor; hands-on, closed Mon–Tue. (The ferry ride is covered too.)

## Citywide
- [NYPL Toddler Story Time](../places/event-storytime-nypl.md) — free, indoor,
  at branches across the city.
