Dated toddler-friendly events on the next few weekends near home. **Compiled

## 🔁 Weekly anchors (reliable, every weekend in season)
- **Smorgasburg** — *Saturdays, 11am–6pm*, Marsha P. Johnson State Park
  (90 Kent Ave). Open-air food market, stroller-friendly. Runs Apr–Oct.
  → [Smorgasburg page](../places/event-smorgasburg.md)
- **Domino Park Greenmarket (GrowNYC)** — *Sundays*, mid-June through late Nov.
  Produce + space to stroll on the waterfront.
  → near [Domino Park](../places/playground-domino-park.md)
- **McCarren / Greenpoint Greenmarket** — *Saturdays, year-round*. Produce,
  baked goods, sometimes live music + cooking demos.
  → at [McCarren Park](../places/playground-mccarren-park.md)
- **Play in the Square @ Domino Square** — *select Sunday mornings, ~10:30am &
  12:30pm*. Free children's performers/workshops (e.g. sing-alongs, musical
  theatre), summer through late September. ⚠️ Confirm which Sundays at
  dominopark.com/events.
