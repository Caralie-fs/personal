Quick playground stops off the **Aberdeen ↔ Williamsburg** drive, in driving
order. **⚡ = ≤5 min detour.** Free NYC Parks unless noted; `verify:` details.

## 🇳🇯 New Jersey (GSP)
- **Cheesequake State Park** ⚡ — exit 120 · trails + lakeside playground *(verify)*
- **Holmdel Park** — near Aberdeen · big playground + free farm animals *(verify)*

## 🗽 Staten Island (440 → I-278)
- **Bloomingdale Park** ⚡ — Rossville · big sensory playground *(verify)*
- **Schmul Park** ⚡ — Travis · swings + play equipment *(verify)*
- **Clove Lakes Park** ⚡ — Clove Rd · playgrounds + ponds *(verify)*
- **Willowbrook Park** — Victory Blvd · playground + accessible carousel *(verify)*
- **Fort Wadsworth** — Verrazzano base · bridge/harbor view (no playground)
- [Staten Island Zoo](../places/attraction-staten-island-zoo.md) — small zoo, free Wed 2–4pm *(real outing)*
- [SI Children's Museum](../places/museum-si-childrens-museum.md) — hands-on, closed Mon–Tue *(real outing)*

## 🌉 Brooklyn (BQE)
- **Owl's Head Park** ⚡ — Bay Ridge, 1st exit · hilltop playground + views *(verify)*
- **Leif Ericson Playground** ⚡ — Bay Ridge (66th St) · accessible playground *(verify)*
- [Fairytale Island](../places/indoor-play-fairytale-island-bay-ridge.md) — Bay Ridge · play cafe (rain option)
- [Sunset Park / Industry City](../places/neighborhood-sunset-park.md) — 38th St · playground + food hall
- **Coffey Park** ⚡ — Red Hook, Hamilton Ave exit · playground *(verify)*

- **Cobble Hill Park** — Cobble Hill · small leafy playground *(verify)*

- [Domino Park](../places/playground-domino-park.md) — home · waterfront water play
