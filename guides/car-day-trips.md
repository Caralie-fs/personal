Outings that are easiest (or only sensible) **with the car** — grouped into
ready-made days, with parking notes. Drive times are rough from Williamsburg;
all `verify:` the parking specifics before relying on them.

## 🅿️ Best for free / easy parking
- [Queens County Farm Museum](../places/attraction-queens-county-farm.md) —
  Glen Oaks · **free admission + free lot** · ≈30–45 min. The easiest car day.
- [Wave Hill](../places/garden-wave-hill.md) — Riverdale · **free on-site lot**
  (limited) · ≈30–40 min. Calm garden + river views.
- [Flushing Meadows–Corona Park](../places/attraction-flushing-meadows.md) —
  Corona · park lots · ≈30–40 min. Free, huge, playgrounds + fountain.

## 🗽 Ready-made days

### Flushing Meadows (Queens) — science + animals + park
[NYSCI](../places/museum-nysci.md) → [Queens Zoo](../places/attraction-queens-zoo.md)
→ [Flushing Meadows–Corona Park](../places/attraction-flushing-meadows.md)
playground/fountain. All in one park; lots on-site. ≈30–40 min drive.

### Bronx Park — zoo + gardens
[Bronx Zoo](../places/attraction-bronx-zoo.md) and
[New York Botanical Garden](../places/garden-nybg.md) are next door to each
other; parking at both. Pick one per visit with little kids — each is a big day.
≈30–40 min.

### Prospect Park (Brooklyn) — carousel + zoo + garden
[Prospect Park Children's Corner](../places/attraction-prospect-park.md) +
[Brooklyn Botanic Garden](../places/garden-brooklyn-botanic.md). Street parking
around the park; BBG has a paid lot. ≈15–20 min — barely a "trip."

### Eastern Queens — farm day
[Queens County Farm Museum](../places/attraction-queens-county-farm.md) —
free animals + free parking, an easy half-day. ≈30–45 min.

## Notes
- For Manhattan spots (AMNH, Central Park Zoo), **leave the car home** — parking
  is scarce/expensive; transit is easier. Those are tagged accordingly.
